## App name

Qurabic

## Short description

- Indonesia: Baca Al-Qur’an, pahami kata dan akar dalam Indonesia atau English. (66 characters)
- English: Read the Quran word by word. Explore roots in English or Indonesian. (68 characters)

## Full description — Indonesia

Baca Al-Qur’an sambil menelusuri kata Arab, akar kata, dan bentuk bahasanya. Qurabic menyediakan antarmuka Indonesia dan English, teks Arab, terjemahan ayat Kemenag RI dan Saheeh International, pencarian ayat dan kata, serta pemutar tilawah qari manusia.

Di halaman kata, lihat glos berdasarkan ayat, bentuk yang tercatat di korpus, dan rujukan kamus klasik saat tersedia. Di halaman akar, bandingkan keluarga kata dan pemakaiannya di ayat. Pencarian kamus juga mencakup bentuk dan partikel bahasa Arab. Tiap sumber diberi nama; glos ayat bukan definisi kamus akar.

Untuk membaca tanpa koneksi, buka aplikasi saat online lalu simpan paket teks dan terjemahan. Audio diunduh terpisah ke penyimpanan privat aplikasi. Bedah kata, kamus, akar, dan tafsir memerlukan koneksi internet.

## Full description — English

Read the Quran while exploring its Arabic words, roots, and forms. Qurabic offers an Indonesian or English interface, Arabic text, verse translations from Kemenag RI and Saheeh International, verse and word search, and recitations by human readers.

Word pages show verse-based glosses, corpus forms, and classical dictionary references when available. Root pages connect related Quranic forms and their verse usage. The Arabic dictionary also searches forms and particles. Sources are named; a verse gloss is not a dictionary definition of a root.

To read offline, open the app online and save the text and translation pack. Al-Afasy recitation can be downloaded separately to private app storage. Word, dictionary, root, and tafsir study require an internet connection.

## Claims to keep out of the listing until proven

- Fully offline word study, root dictionary, or tafsir. Current offline sync covers Quran text and verse translations; recitations are separate downloads. The reader shell and pack require an initial successful online visit/sync.
- Complete or scholarly reviewed definitions for every root. Root indexes, glosses, and dictionary sources have different coverage and provenance.
- “AI-free” as a blanket claim. Attribute each dataset and do not describe source claims as independent verification.
- “Official Kemenag digital mushaf.” Text and translation sources are identified separately; Qurabic has not established LPMQ approval of its rendered digital layout.
